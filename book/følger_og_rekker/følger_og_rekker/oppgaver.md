:::::::::::::::{exercise-2} Oppgave 2
Regn ut summene nedenfor.

:::::::::::::{part} a
$$
\sum_{n = 1}^5 (2n + 1)
$$


:::::{answer-2}


$$
\sum_{n = 1}^5 (2n + 1) = 35
$$


::::{solution-2}
$$
\begin{align*}
\sum_{n = 1}^5 (2n + 1) &= (2 \cdot 1 + 1) + (2 \cdot 2 + 1) + (2 \cdot 3 + 1) + (2 \cdot 4 + 1) + (2 \cdot 5 + 1) \\
&= 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 + 11 \\
\\
&= 35
\end{align*}
$$
::::
:::::

:::::::::::::


:::::::::::::{part} b
$$
\sum_{n = 2}^4 (n^2 - 1)
$$


:::::{answer-2}
$$
\sum_{n = 2}^4 (n^2 - 1) = 26
$$


::::{solution-2}
$$
\begin{align*}
\sum_{n = 2}^4 (n^2 - 1) &= (2^2 - 1) + (3^2 - 1) + (4^2 - 1) \\
&= 3 + 8 + 15 \\
\\
&= 26
\end{align*}
$$
::::
:::::



:::::::::::::

:::::::::::::{part} c
$$
\sum_{n = 0}^3 2^n
$$


:::::{answer-2}

$$
\sum_{n = 0}^3 2^n = 15
$$

::::{solution-2}
$$
\begin{align*}
\sum_{n = 0}^3 2^n &= 2^0 + 2^1 + 2^2 + 2^3 \\
&= 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 \\
\\
&= 15
\end{align*}
$$
